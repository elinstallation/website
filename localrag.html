Local RAG Model

A local Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipeline that lets you chat with your own documents, built with Ollama and LlamaIndex. Documents are chunked, embedded, and indexed locally, then retrieved at query time to ground an LLM's answers in your own data — all without sending anything to an external API.
